01

Every fact carries its quote

A fact only enters the ledger if the exact sentence can be found in the source. Numbers must match digit for digit.

02

"No record" is an answer

If your records don't show it, Raymond says so and tells you where it looked. It does not guess.

03

It asks when you could mean two things

Two Imrans, two open invoices: it asks which one, instead of picking.

04

Dates and sums are done by code

"Next Friday" is resolved by a calendar, not by a model's arithmetic. Clashes are found by comparing times.

05

Email can't give it orders

Text inside a message is treated as data. Hidden instructions addressed to "the AI assistant" are flagged to you as suspicious, never followed.

06

You approve every action

Sending, moving, paying, deleting: each one waits as a card with the exact content, for you to approve or reject.

Questions

      

        
Is it as capable as the big cloud assistants?
For open-ended writing and general knowledge, no: a model that fits on a desktop graphics card is smaller. For questions about your own business it doesn't need to be, because the answers come from your records, with the quote attached, not from the model's memory.

        
Which mail does it work with?
Microsoft 365 and Outlook mail and calendar, plus any folders of documents you point it at (PDF, Word, Excel, text, meeting recordings).

        
Can it send email on its own?
No. It prepares the email and shows it to you. It goes out only when you approve that exact text.

        
What happens when it's wrong?
Every answer shows the sentence it came from, so you can check it in one glance. If the source says something different from the answer, that's a bug we want to hear about.

        
Which computers does it run on?
Windows 10 or 11 and Linux, with an NVIDIA graphics card that has 8 GB of memory, and Macs with Apple Silicon (M1 or later) and 16 GB of memory. Each needs 16 GB of RAM, about 20 GB of free disk and Docker. The model runs on the graphics chip, which is what keeps answers fast without sending anything off the machine.
